Visiting this website

This website is hosted using GitHub Pages. To deliver pages and protect its infrastructure, GitHub processes technical information such as your IP address and request information. More information about its processing and international data transfers is available in the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Our purpose in providing the website is to make information and resources available reliably and securely. The legal basis for this processing is our legitimate interest under Article 6(1)(f) GDPR.

Cookies, analytics and external content

This website does not use advertising or analytics scripts, set its own cookies, or store filter choices in your browser. The EO Data DB filters run locally in your browser. Images, styles and scripts are delivered with the website.

Links to YouTube, social networks, software repositories and other external resources take you to the respective provider. Those services process information according to their own privacy policies when you visit them. Videos are linked rather than automatically embedded.

Contacting us

If you contact us by email or telephone, we use the information you provide to respond to your enquiry. The legal basis is Article 6(1)(b) GDPR for enquiries relating to a contract, or Article 6(1)(f) GDPR for other correspondence. We retain correspondence only for as long as needed to handle the enquiry or meet applicable retention obligations.
